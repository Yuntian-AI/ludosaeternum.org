Game Overview

Madalin Stunt Cars Pro is an adrenaline-fueled stunt driving game that puts you in control of high-performance vehicles on specially designed stunt tracks. Perform spectacular jumps, flips, rolls, and drifts as you push the limits of what's possible with these meticulously modeled cars.

With realistic physics, multiple detailed environments, and a diverse collection of powerful cars, Madalin Stunt Cars Pro offers endless opportunities for creative stunts and exploration. Challenge yourself to pull off the most extreme tricks or race against friends in multiplayer mode!

Game Controls

Basic Driving

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/D or Left/Right Arrows: Steering
  • Space: Handbrake (for drifting)

Stunts & Tricks

  • Ctrl + Arrow Keys: Perform flips
  • Shift + Arrow Keys: Barrel rolls
  • Use ramps for big air time
  • Combine keys for complex stunts

Car Selection

  • ESC: Open main menu
  • Select from various car models
  • Each car has unique handling
  • Try different cars for various stunts

Camera & Controls

  • C: Change camera view
  • R: Reset car position
  • M: Toggle map
  • Tab: Show/hide player list

Stunt Driving Tips

Beginner Tips

  • Master the basic controls before attempting complex stunts
  • Start with slower cars to get familiar with the physics
  • Use handbrake (Space) to initiate drifts around corners
  • Practice landing jumps properly to avoid vehicle damage
  • Use R key to reset if your car gets stuck or flips over
  • Explore each map to find the best stunt opportunities

Advanced Techniques

  • Time your acceleration perfectly when approaching ramps
  • Combine multiple key inputs for complex mid-air maneuvers
  • Use different cars for different stunt types - lighter cars for flips, heavier cars for stability
  • Chain multiple stunts together for impressive combos
  • Master weight transfer techniques to maintain control during jumps
  • Learn each map's unique features and stunt opportunities
  • Use camera angles strategically to plan and execute stunts
