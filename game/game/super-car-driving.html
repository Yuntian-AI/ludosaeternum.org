Game Overview

Super Car Driving is an exhilarating racing and driving simulation game that puts you behind the wheel of some of the world's most powerful supercars. Experience high-speed thrills as you navigate challenging tracks, master perfect drifts, and push your driving skills to the limit.

With realistic physics, stunning graphics, and a variety of cars to unlock, Super Car Driving offers an immersive automotive experience. Compete for the best lap times, earn rewards, and become the ultimate supercar driver!

Controls

Driving

  • WASD or arrow keys for vehicle control
  • W/Up Arrow to accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow to brake/reverse
  • A/D or Left/Right Arrows to steer

Special Moves

  • Spacebar for handbrake/drift
  • Shift key for nitro boost
  • Control timing for perfect drifts

Camera & View

  • C key to toggle camera views
  • First-person and third-person options
  • R key to reset car position if stuck

Game Menu

  • ESC key to pause the game
  • Access settings and car selection
  • View track information and records

Driving Tips

Beginner Tips

  • Start with slower cars to master the handling basics
  • Brake before entering corners, not during them
  • Use gentle steering inputs to prevent spinouts
  • Complete tutorial races to earn starter credits
  • Focus on clean driving before attempting drifts

Advanced Techniques

  • Master the perfect drift timing for maximum points
  • Use handbrake and throttle together for controlled slides
  • Optimize racing lines to achieve fastest lap times
  • Save nitro boosts for long straightaways
  • Learn each track's unique shortcut opportunities
  • Practice weight transfer techniques for better cornering

Car Upgrade System

As you complete races and challenges, you'll earn credits to upgrade your cars and unlock new vehicles:

Engine Upgrades

Increase horsepower, acceleration, and top speed for better straight-line performance

Handling Improvements

Upgrade suspension, tires, and brakes for better cornering and stability

Aerodynamics

Add spoilers, diffusers, and body kits to improve downforce and high-speed stability

Nitro Systems

Enhance nitrous oxide systems for more powerful and longer-lasting speed boosts

Customization

Personalize your cars with custom paint jobs, decals, rims, and interior upgrades

Vehicle Unlocks

Earn achievements to unlock premium supercars with unique handling characteristics and special abilities

Create your ultimate supercar by balancing upgrades to match your driving style. Each track demands different vehicle setups for optimal performance!
